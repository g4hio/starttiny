A study timer without unnecessary complexity

StartTiny is a lightweight study timer for students who want to begin a session without setting up a complicated productivity system. Add a task, choose a focus period, and start.

Useful for different study sessions

  • 10/2: a low-pressure quick start.
  • 25/5: a familiar Pomodoro-style session.
  • 40/10: a longer revision block.
  • 50/10: a deeper study session.
  • Custom: choose your own focus and break periods.

Keep your study session clear

Tasks can have priorities and optional deadlines. Smart Mode puts deadline-driven work first, while manual sorting lets you decide whether to organize by deadline, priority, newest, oldest, or completion.

The brain dump gives you a place to write down unrelated thoughts without opening another app.
