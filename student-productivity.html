Free Student Productivity Tool

StartTiny is a lightweight student productivity tool: tasks, optional deadlines, a flexible student focus timer, private notes, sorting, and a Help Me Start prompt. It is free, open source, and does not require an account.

Open the student productivity tool

Built for schoolwork without extra complexity

Many productivity apps ask students to plan everything. StartTiny focuses on getting the next assignment started. Add homework, essays, reading, coding, research, or exam revision as tasks. Give high-priority work a deadline if it has one. Then start a timer.

Smart sorting can surface overdue and due-soon work first. Manual sorting is there when you want control.

What students can keep in one place

  • Assignments with priority and optional due dates
  • A study timer that matches energy: 10, 25, 40, or 50 minutes
  • A private brain dump for reminders, worries, and off-topic ideas
  • Focus mode when the rest of the page is noise

Nothing is uploaded. Notes and tasks stay in the browser. That makes it usable in a library, classroom, or shared computer without creating a login.

Also useful beyond school

The same student productivity setup works for internships, applications, personal projects, and adult study. StartTiny is for students, professionals, and anyone who needs a smaller first step rather than a heavier planner.
