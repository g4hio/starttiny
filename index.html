Stop procrastinating. Start with one tiny step.

StartTiny helps reduce the friction of getting started by combining a flexible focus timer, tasks, deadlines, task sorting, a private brain dump, and small prompts for the next action.

FOR STUDENTS

Use it for homework, revision, essays, coding, reading, exam preparation, and long-term assignments.

FOR ADULTS & WORK

Use it for work projects, writing, programming, applications, personal projects, household tasks, and anything you keep putting off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, backend, advertising, or analytics. Your tasks, notes, and settings stay in this browser.

FREE & OPEN SOURCE

Inspect, fork, modify, and contribute through the public GitHub repository.
