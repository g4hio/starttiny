Free Pomodoro Alternative

Classic Pomodoro uses 25 minutes of work and 5 minutes of break. That helps many people. It does not help everyone, every day. StartTiny is a free Pomodoro alternative: keep 25/5, or switch to 10/2, 40/10, 50/10, or any custom pair.

Try the flexible timer

Why people look for a Pomodoro alternative

25 minutes can feel too long when you are avoiding a task. It can also feel too short when you are already in flow. A good timer should fit the person and the work, not force one interval forever.

StartTiny still supports the familiar 25/5 student focus timer. The difference is that you are not locked into it.

Presets you can use immediately

  • 10 / 2 — a tiny start when 25 minutes feels like a wall.
  • 25 / 5 — standard Pomodoro, if that already works.
  • 40 / 10 and 50 / 10 — longer deep-work blocks.
  • Custom — any focus and break length you choose.

More than a timer

A Pomodoro alternative is more useful when the task is visible and distractions have a place to go. StartTiny adds tasks, priorities, optional deadlines, sorting, a private brain dump, Help Me Start, energy check, and focus mode. It is free, open source, and local-first.
