A free anti-procrastination app that makes starting easier

Starting is often harder than the actual task. StartTiny is a free procrastination timer and student focus timer that reduces that first barrier: choose one task, pick a manageable session, and begin.

FLEXIBLE FOCUS TIMER

Use 10/2, 25/5, 40/10, 50/10, or create your own focus and break lengths. It works as a study timer, work timer, or Pomodoro alternative.

TASKS AND DEADLINES

Add tasks, priorities, and optional deadlines. Sort by deadline, priority, newest, oldest, completion, or Smart Mode.

PRIVATE BRAIN DUMP

Write down reminders, distractions, ideas, or worries so they stop competing for attention. Notes stay in this browser.

HELP ME START

When a task feels overwhelming, StartTiny turns it into a smaller first action instead of asking for more motivation.

FOR STUDENTS

Use this student productivity tool for homework, revision, essays, coding, reading, exam preparation, and long-term assignments.

FOR ADULTS & WORK

Use it for work projects, writing, programming, applications, personal projects, household tasks, and anything you keep putting off.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No account, backend, advertising, or analytics. Your tasks, notes, and settings stay in this browser using local storage.

FREE & OPEN SOURCE
