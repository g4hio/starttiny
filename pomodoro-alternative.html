Free Pomodoro Alternative

StartTiny is a flexible Pomodoro alternative for people who want custom focus and break lengths instead of one fixed interval.

Open StartTiny

Why this can help

You can use classic 25/5 sessions or try 10/2, 40/10, 50/10, or any custom combination. The goal is to make the timer fit the task and the person.

StartTiny keeps the process simple: decide what to work on, choose a manageable session, and begin. You can add a deadline or priority when it helps, and keep unrelated thoughts in the private brain dump.

StartTiny features

  • Flexible focus and break timer
  • Tasks with optional priorities and deadlines
  • Smart and manual task sorting
  • Individual private brain-dump notes
  • Help Me Start prompts
  • Focus mode and energy check
  • No account, backend, ads, or analytics
