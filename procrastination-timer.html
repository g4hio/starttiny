Free Procrastination Timer for Students & Adults

StartTiny is a free, open-source procrastination timer that helps you begin with a manageable focus session. Choose 10/2, 25/5, 40/10, 50/10, or create your own timer.

Open StartTiny

Why this can help

A simple timer is useful when the hard part is starting. StartTiny combines focus sessions with tasks, deadlines, and a private brain dump so you can move from intention to action.

StartTiny keeps the process simple: decide what to work on, choose a manageable session, and begin. You can add a deadline or priority when it helps, and keep unrelated thoughts in the private brain dump.

StartTiny features

  • Flexible focus and break timer
  • Tasks with optional priorities and deadlines
  • Smart and manual task sorting
  • Individual private brain-dump notes
  • Help Me Start prompts
  • Focus mode and energy check
  • No account, backend, ads, or analytics
