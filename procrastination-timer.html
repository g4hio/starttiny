Free Procrastination Timer for Students & Adults

A procrastination timer is useful when the hard part is starting, not finishing. StartTiny is a free, open-source timer that helps you begin with one manageable focus session instead of waiting until you feel ready.

Open the free procrastination timer

What a procrastination timer actually does

Most people do not need a more complicated productivity system. They need a smaller first step. A short timed session reduces the decision of “how long should I work?” and turns a large task into “work until the timer ends.”

StartTiny is built for that moment. Add the task, choose 10, 25, 40, or 50 minutes, or set your own length, then start. If other thoughts appear, put them in the private brain dump so they do not steal the session.

Which timer length to choose

  • 10 / 2 — when you feel tired, stuck, or overwhelmed. Ten minutes is short enough to start.
  • 25 / 5 — a familiar student focus timer for homework, revision, or writing.
  • 40 / 10 or 50 / 10 — when you already have momentum and want a deeper work block.
  • Custom — if a fixed Pomodoro interval does not fit the task.

How to use StartTiny as a procrastination timer

  1. Write one task. Not the whole project, just the next piece of work.
  2. Pick a session that feels slightly too small, not heroic.
  3. Press start before you feel motivated.
  4. If a distraction appears, park it in notes and return to the task.
  5. Count the session as success even if it was short.

This works for students, professionals, and everyday tasks: homework, emails you have been avoiding, applications, household jobs, coding, and reading.
